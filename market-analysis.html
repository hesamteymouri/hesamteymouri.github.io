Marketing Analysis & Customer Value

Integrating marketing strategy, analytical frameworks, and AI-assisted tools to identify sources of customer value and build competitive advantage. Fall 2026.

Overview

Course Description

This course integrates marketing strategy with the enhancement of value in firms, products, services, and industries. It focuses on competitive and customer metrics and analysis, the development of marketing strategy, and the implementation of strategic marketing plans in business organizations.

The course incorporates current developments in marketing to acquaint students with present-day challenges of marketing activities and serves as a vehicle for the application and integration of marketing concepts, analytical tools, and problem-solving approaches used in developing marketing strategy.

Objectives

Course Learning Outcomes

✓Analyze markets, industries, competitors, and customers using contemporary analytical frameworks
✓Identify sources of customer value and competitive advantage
✓Evaluate market opportunities using qualitative and quantitative evidence
✓Develop customer-centric marketing strategies
✓Apply marketing metrics to support strategic decision making
✓Use AI as an analytical tool while critically evaluating the quality of its output
✓Design and justify strategic marketing recommendations
✓Communicate marketing analyses and recommendations professionally
Topics

What Students Learn

Customer Value

Students explore what value means from the customer's perspective and how firms create, deliver, and capture it across different business contexts.

AI as a Strategic Analyst

Students use AI tools to generate synthetic customer panels, simulate qualitative research, and produce market insights — learning to verify every output against real evidence.

Customer Journey Design

Students map touchpoints across the full customer journey, identifying friction points and opportunities for value creation and experience improvement.

Industry Attractiveness

Students apply frameworks to assess competitive dynamics, market structure, and the strategic forces shaping industry profitability.

Customer Insights

Students gather and interpret qualitative and quantitative customer data — including AI-generated synthetic research — to inform strategic decisions.

Market Segmentation

Students identify and evaluate distinct customer segments, using both traditional frameworks and AI-assisted methods to uncover meaningful differences in needs and behavior.

Branding & Positioning

Students analyze brand strategy and positioning decisions, evaluating how firms differentiate themselves and communicate value to target segments.

Competitive Analysis & Advantage

Students assess competitors' strategies and identify sustainable sources of competitive advantage using structured analytical tools.

Value Proposition Design

Students develop and test value propositions for their chosen companies, validating them against synthetic customer profiles and real market evidence.

Customer Lifetime Value & Customer Equity

Students apply CLV frameworks and customer equity models to assess the long-term value of customer relationships and inform strategic investment decisions.

Pedagogy

Studio-Based Learning

Each session follows a studio structure: the instructor teaches the week's concept (30–40 min), followed by a break, then teams work on an assigned Harvard Business School case and present their findings to the class. Teams submit a one-page brief on Canvas after each session.

Across the semester, student teams build a complete AI-driven strategy report for a company of their choice — using AI to generate synthetic customer panels, simulate qualitative research, segment the market, test value propositions, and produce strategic recommendations. Every AI output must be verified against real public evidence, teaching students to use AI critically as an analytical tool rather than a shortcut.
